Kerr black hole simulations

Interactive visualizations of spinning black holes: analytic Kerr shadows today, and a planned comparison of Newtonian versus general-relativistic timelike orbits.

Analytic Kerr shadow

3D Kerr geometry with event horizon, photon spheres, and the black-hole shadow from Bardeen (1973) and Teo (2003) geodesic radii.

Live · open the interactive viewer

Timelike orbits

Side-by-side Newtonian and GR orbits for massive particles around black holes — scaffolding for a future simulation.
